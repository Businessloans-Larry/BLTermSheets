Offers 1 & 3 · Lender A

Best fit for drawing and paying off inside 30 days

Through the fourth weekly payment — roughly the first 30 days — the payoff is simply your remaining principal. Draw $500,000 on Offer 1 and close it out at day 28 and the whole thing costs $10,000 ($510,000 all‑in). At payment 5 it switches to a discounted‑balance basis and the all‑in jumps to $550,913, so the first month is where the value is.

Largest lines on the table — up to $545,000 — and no origination or draw fee.

Offer 4 · Lender B

Better for drawing and holding

No 30‑day cliff: the payoff discount comes down evenly across the full 15 months, so there is no penalty for keeping the money out. The trade‑off is size and cost — $300,000 is their maximum line, and the first draw carries $15,000 in fees.

Lowest weekly payment on the sheet at a full draw: $7,175.

Offers 2, 5, 6 & 7 · Lender C

Better fit for a lump-sum deposit

These are term loans, not lines — the full amount hits your account at closing. The right pick if you actually need all of it up front and expect to hold it longer than 60 days: past the first month the two lines above lose their cheap‑exit advantage, while these price evenly across the whole term.

Largest approval on the sheet at $814,000 — and we reimburse the full 2.5% origination fee if it funds in September.

How These Offers Work

  • Offers 1, 3 and 4 are revolving draw lines — you are only charged on what you actually draw. Offers 2, 5, 6 and 7 are term loans — the full amount funds at closing.
  • Minimum opening draw on the lines: $50,000 (Offer 1), $54,500 (Offer 3), $15,000 (Offer 4).
  • Offers 1 & 3 carry no origination fee and no draw fee. Offer 4 carries a 2.5% draw fee plus a one-time 2.5%-of-limit origination fee. Offers 2, 5, 6 & 7 carry a 2.5% origination fee — reimbursed by us if they fund in September.
  • Every payment on this sheet is weekly. On Offers 1 & 3 the payback period is an estimate, because payments are a set percentage of receipts.
  • Every offer can be paid off early at a discount. Open the early payoff schedules above for the exact figure at any point in the term.

The September origination-fee reimbursement is paid by BusinessLoans.com, not the lender, and applies only if the loan funds on or before September 30, 2026. Actual amounts may differ due to funding date, bank holidays, missed or early payments, additional draws, and other factors.
