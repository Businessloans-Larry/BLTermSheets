Use it

Open a deal in Zoho and click Submit to Figure. Figure opens with everything filled: property address, ownership, occupancy, income, business details, lead source, and the consent box.

You type the date of birth — a green box shows it, and the cursor is already in the field. Figure's date box only accepts real typing. Then review and click Continue.

Good to know
  • The deal data travels in the part of the link after the #, which browsers never send to Figure's servers — it stays in your browser.
  • Nothing is submitted automatically. You always review and click Continue yourself.
  • The script only runs on Figure's lead portal. It does nothing anywhere else.
  • Updates arrive on their own — Tampermonkey re-checks this page periodically.
