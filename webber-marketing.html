Disclosure. This term sheet is for illustration purposes only and is not a commitment to lend. Subject to the ISO agreement with each funder and satisfaction of that lender's stipulations above. The total funding amount may not equal the amount deposited to your business checking account. Payments are set as a specified percentage of estimated receipts, so the number of payments and the payback period shown are estimates and may vary with actual receipts. Early-payoff figures are the funder's own quoted amounts, scale with the draw shown, and assume payments are made as scheduled; accelerated payoff is offered at the funder's sole discretion and may be accepted or rejected. The simple interest rate shown is the total cost of capital at that payoff point divided by the draw amount; it is not annualized and is not an APR. Offer 3 is a line of credit that amortizes at a fixed rate: its schedule assumes the full limit is drawn at once and every payment is made on time, and its remaining balance may be paid in full at any time with the remaining interest waived. Actual amounts may differ due to funding date, bank holidays, missed or early payments, and other factors.
